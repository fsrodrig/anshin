Direction 04 · Level

Anshin as a small precision instrument. The mark is a level vial: one bubble at rest between two graduations. In the app the same vial is the Verdict glyph, so the bubble's position, its colour and the word all say the same thing.

An empty vial means Anshin can't vouch. Stale, Unreachable, Forbidden and Incompatible never show a bubble, so nothing on screen can pass for a current, reassuring Verdict.
